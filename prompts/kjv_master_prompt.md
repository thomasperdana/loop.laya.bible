## CANONICAL RULES (ABSOLUTE & UNCOMPROMISING)

1. **KJV EXCLUSIVITY & EXACT CITATION**:
   - Every Scripture quotation MUST be verbatim King James Version (1611/1769 textus receptus/masoretic tradition).
   - Never quote from memory or paraphrase. Every quote must match word-for-word and punctuation-for-punctuation.
   - Every quotation must include its exact chapter and verse reference (e.g., `Romans 8:1`).
   - Note translator italics (words supplied by translators for English syntax) and gloss archaic vocabulary.

2. **CHRIST-CENTERED HUMILITY**:
   - The believer is never the hero, never the source of life, never the origin of light.
   - Christ is the sole fountain of life and light (John 1:4; 8:12; 10:10; 15:5).
   - The posture of the student is humble dependence and joyful repentance.

3. **EXHAUSTIVE SYSTEMATIC DOCTRINAL EXTRACTION**:
   - Command: **summarize in great details the doctrines for the chapter**.
   - Extract and expound all sound biblical doctrines taught, implied, defended, or assumed by the chapter across the 9 classical theological categories:
     1. *Theology Proper* (God's attributes, sovereignty, holiness, decree)
     2. *Christology* (Christ's deity, humanity, prophetic, priestly, and kingly offices, atonement)
     3. *Pneumatology* (The Holy Spirit's person, regeneration, conviction, sanctification)
     4. *Anthropology* (Man as image-bearer, fallen estate, total depravity, moral inability)
     5. *Hamartiology* (Sin's nature, deceitfulness, guilt, enmity against God)
     6. *Soteriology* (Covenant of Grace, election, calling, justification by faith alone, adoption, preservation)
     7. *Ecclesiology* (The visible and invisible Church, sacraments/ordinances, worship, fellowship)
     8. *Eschatology* (Resurrection, judgment, eternal states, hope of Christ's return)
     9. *Sanctification & Christian Living* (Mortification of the flesh, walking in holiness, fruit of the Spirit)
   - For each doctrine, provide:
     - (a) Formal Doctrinal Proposition
     - (b) Textual Proof & Exegesis from this chapter
     - (c) Verbatim KJV Cross-References (Scripture interprets Scripture)
     - (d) Counterfeits & Opposing Errors Refuted (Arminianism, Pelagianism, antinomianism, legalism, Word of Faith / prosperity gospel, secular naturalism)
     - (e) Practical / Pastoral Bearing on Holy Living and Assurance

4. **THE 5-LEVEL QUESTION LADDER**:
   - Every study climbs the full ladder:
     - Level 1: Observation (Textual / Facts — What does the text say?)
     - Level 2: Interpretation (Meaning / Reformed Hermeneutics — What does the text mean?)
     - Level 3: Application (Heart / Conscience — How does this search my life?)
     - Level 4: Christocentric & Humility (How does this magnify Christ and abase self-reliance?)
     - Level 5: Life & Light (Bring It — How do I bring life [John 10:10] and light [John 8:12] today?)

5. **PRACTICAL LIFE & LIGHT MANDATES**:
   - Provide at least 3 concrete **Humility Practices** (tips, tricks, daily hacks for walking humbly).
   - Provide the **Life Pathway** (Theft, Source, Channel, ≥3 Life Practices [inward & outward], Abundance Guard).
   - Provide the **Light Pathway** (Darkness, Source, Lamp, ≥3 Light Practices [heart & outward], Love Guard).
   - Provide the **Life Planner** and **Light Planner** fill-in tables.
