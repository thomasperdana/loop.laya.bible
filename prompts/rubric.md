# Berean Council Bible Study Evaluation Rubric

This rubric defines the pass/fail and quality standards for evaluating an in-depth KJV chapter Bible study and workbook generated by the Berean Council loop.

## Standard Checkers & Thresholds

| Stage | Checker Name | Type | Pass Threshold | Target Metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verse Checker | Deterministic | 0 quote mismatches | 100% exact KJV match for referenced quotations |
| 2 | Structural Conformance | Deterministic / AST | 19 / 19 sections present | All 19 points, 7 council seats, 5 question levels, 9 doctrinal heads |
| 3 | Laya System 1 Decision Engine | Non-autoregressive model | Composite pass | `doctrinal_soundness`: score ≥ 2 ("sound and deep")<br>`kjv_faithfulness`: noul ≥ 0.85<br>`life_light_practicality`: choice == "actionable"<br>`humility_posture`: choice == "Christ-magnifying" |
| 4 | Human Checkpoint | Rung 2 Suggest / Approval | Discretionary | Leader doctrinal review before printing/teaching |

## Detailed Evaluation Criteria

### Criterion 1: Verbatim KJV Scripture Integrity
- **5 (Exemplary)**: Every quotation marked with a reference (e.g. `Genesis 1:1`) matches the canonical KJV text letter-for-letter and punctuation-for-punctuation. Archaic words are accurately glossed. Translator italics are faithfully annotated. No modern paraphrases or contemporary translations substituted.
- **3 (Acceptable)**: Quotes are substantially KJV with minor punctuation variance that does not alter theological sense.
- **1 (Unacceptable)**: Misquotes, non-KJV translations (NIV/ESV/NLT) slipped in without attribution, or fabricated verse text.

### Criterion 2: Exhaustive Systematic Doctrinal Summarization
- **5 (Exemplary)**: Expounds sound doctrine across all applicable heads (Theology Proper, Christology, Pneumatology, Anthropology, Hamartiology, Soteriology, Ecclesiology, Eschatology, Sanctification). Each doctrine includes a formal proposition, textual proof from the chapter, verbatim KJV cross-references, error refutations, and pastoral bearing.
- **3 (Acceptable)**: Covers multiple doctrinal heads but treats some superficially or omits explicit error refutations.
- **1 (Unacceptable)**: Generic devotional summary without systematic doctrinal categorization; false doctrine or unbiblical speculation present.

### Criterion 3: Christocentricity & Humility Posture
- **5 (Exemplary)**: Christ is magnified as the sole source of life (John 10:10) and light (John 8:12). The believer is humbled as an earthen vessel and channel (John 15:5). Contains at least 3 concrete, daily humility practices.
- **3 (Acceptable)**: Mentions Christ and humility, but occasionally slips into moralism or self-reliant exhortations.
- **1 (Unacceptable)**: Self-centered, prosperity-oriented, legalistic, or treats human effort as the origin of spiritual life.

### Criterion 4: 5-Level Question Ladder Rigor
- **5 (Exemplary)**: Climbs all five distinct levels: Observation, Interpretation, Application, Christocentric/Humility, Life & Light. Every question is open-ended, thought-provoking, and includes write-in lines.
- **3 (Acceptable)**: Questions are present but some levels are blurred or questions are overly simplistic yes/no queries.
- **1 (Unacceptable)**: Missing question levels, closed questions, or questions unrelated to the text.

### Criterion 5: Actionable Life & Light Pathways
- **5 (Exemplary)**: Complete Life Pathway (Theft, Source, Channel, 3 Practices, Abundance Guard) and Light Pathway (Darkness, Source, Lamp, 3 Practices, Love Guard). Interactive write-in tables with specific columns (Person, Need, Action, Date).
- **3 (Acceptable)**: Pathways present but action steps lack specificity or guards are omitted.
- **1 (Unacceptable)**: Absent or reduced to vague positive thinking.
